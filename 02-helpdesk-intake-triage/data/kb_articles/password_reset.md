# How to reset your password

You can reset your own password without waiting for IT: go to accounts.company.com/reset, enter your work email, and follow the link sent to your personal recovery email (set this up in advance under Account Settings if you haven't already). The reset link expires after 15 minutes. If you don't have a recovery email configured, or the reset page won't load, submit a ticket and include your employee ID so we can verify your identity manually — this typically takes under 30 minutes during business hours.
