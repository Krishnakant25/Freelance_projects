# Employee Handbook

## Leave Policy

Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid vacation per calendar year, credited monthly at a rate of 1.5 days. Unused vacation days roll over up to a maximum of 10 days into the following year; any balance above that is forfeited on January 1st. Vacation requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance through the HR portal.

Employees are also entitled to 10 paid sick days per year, which do not roll over. A doctor's note is required for sick leave lasting more than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 12 weeks paid for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks paid for the secondary caregiver, available to employees who have completed at least 6 months of service.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are evaluated on a role-by-role basis. All remote employees must be reachable during core hours, 10am-3pm in their local time zone.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses under $75 can be self-approved and submitted directly for reimbursement. Expenses over $75 require manager pre-approval. All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date, with itemized receipts attached. Reimbursements are processed within 10 business days of approval.

## Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation of any kind are grounds for immediate termination. Employees who witness or experience a violation should report it to HR or through the anonymous ethics hotline.
