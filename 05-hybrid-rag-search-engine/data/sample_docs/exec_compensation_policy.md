# Executive Compensation Policy (Restricted — Management Only)

## Bonus Structure

Executive bonuses are calculated as a percentage of base salary tied to company-wide EBITDA targets: 20% of base salary at 90% of target, scaling linearly to 60% of base salary at 120% of target or above. Bonuses are paid annually in Q1 following the close of the fiscal year, contingent on the executive being employed on the payout date.

## Equity Grants

New executive hires receive an initial equity grant equivalent to 0.5%-2% of fully diluted shares, vesting over 4 years with a 1-year cliff. Refresh grants are issued annually based on performance review outcomes and are approved by the compensation committee.

## Severance Terms

Executives terminated without cause are entitled to 6 months of base salary continuation and 12 months of accelerated equity vesting. Executives who resign voluntarily or are terminated for cause receive no severance beyond what is legally required.
