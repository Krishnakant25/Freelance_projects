# Product FAQ

## Pricing

The Starter plan is $29/month and includes up to 5 team members and 10GB of storage. The Growth plan is $99/month and includes up to 25 team members, 100GB of storage, and priority support. The Enterprise plan is custom-priced and includes unlimited team members, dedicated infrastructure, and a named account manager.

All plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start the trial. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from the billing settings page.

## Data Export

Customers on all plans can export their data at any time in CSV or JSON format from the Settings > Data Export page. Enterprise customers can additionally request a full database export via their account manager, delivered within 5 business days.

## API Rate Limits

The Starter plan is limited to 100 API requests per minute. The Growth plan is limited to 1,000 requests per minute. Enterprise customers have custom rate limits negotiated per contract. Requests that exceed the limit receive a 429 status code and should be retried with exponential backoff.

## Security

All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3. We are SOC 2 Type II certified, with the audit report available to Enterprise customers under NDA. Two-factor authentication is available on all plans and required for Enterprise accounts.

## Support Channels

Starter and Growth customers get email support with a 24-hour response time. Growth customers also get access to live chat during business hours (9am-6pm ET). Enterprise customers get a dedicated Slack channel with a 1-hour response SLA during business hours.
