Q: What are your opening hours?
A: We're open Monday to Friday, nine in the morning until five in the afternoon. We're closed weekends and public holidays.

Q: Where are you located?
A: We're at 42 Northside Avenue, just past the library. There's free parking behind the building, and we're a five minute walk from the train station.

Q: How much does a check-up cost?
A: A standard check-up is eighty pounds, which includes a full examination and any necessary x-rays. A scale and polish with the hygienist is sixty pounds.

Q: Do you take insurance?
A: Yes, we work with most major insurers including Bupa, AXA and Vitality. Bring your policy details to your first appointment and we'll handle the paperwork.

Q: Do you accept new patients?
A: Yes, we're currently accepting new patients. Your first visit will be a full assessment, which takes about forty five minutes.

Q: What should I do if I have a dental emergency?
A: If you're in severe pain or have had an injury, call us and we'll fit you in the same day where possible. Outside opening hours, please contact your local urgent care service.

Q: Do you treat children?
A: Yes, we see patients of all ages. Children under eighteen are seen free of charge when a parent is registered with us.

Q: How long does a filling take?
A: A standard filling appointment takes about thirty minutes. More complex work may need a longer appointment, and we'll let you know when booking.

Q: Can I pay in instalments?
A: For treatment over five hundred pounds we offer interest-free payment plans over six or twelve months. Ask at reception for details.

Q: What happens if I need to cancel?
A: Just let us know at least twenty four hours in advance and there's no charge. Cancellations inside twenty four hours may incur a small fee.

Q: Do I need a referral?
A: No referral is needed for general dentistry. For specialist orthodontic work we may refer you on after an initial assessment.

Q: Is parking available?
A: Yes, there's free parking for patients in the car park behind the building. There are two accessible bays next to the main entrance.
